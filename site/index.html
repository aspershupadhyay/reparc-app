On your Wi-Fi

Turn it on for 15 minutes to 2 hours. Your phone becomes an MCP server on your home network. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code, or Claude Desktop on your laptop connect with a one-time key. No internet hop at all.

claude mcp add --transport http reparc \
  http://192.168.1.20:8765/mcp \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer rpl_…"

Plain HTTP inside your network: use trusted Wi-Fi only. Cloud apps like claude.ai cannot reach it — use the gateway.

☁

Anywhere, through your gateway

One click deploys the open-source gateway into your own free Vercel account. claude.ai, ChatGPT, and any remote-MCP app sign in with OAuth; you approve them on your phone by matching a 4-letter code.

▲ Deploy your gateway

Start setup in the app (Me → AI connections) so it can make your owner key first.

  1. Your AI appasks a question over HTTPS
  2. Your gatewayholds it ~seconds, encrypted with your key
  3. Your phonechecks your switches, answers from local data

Privacy you can check

We built it so we can't see your data.

No account, no Reparc server. Records live in a database on your phone.
AI is off by default. Workouts, meals, health, and plan suggestions are separate switches. Access turns itself off.
AI can't write. Plans arrive as proposals. Only your tap saves them.
The gateway is yours. Your Vercel, your Upstash, your key. It stores no records; relay messages are sealed with AES-256-GCM and expire in a minute.
No analytics, no ads, no crash trackers. Not in the app, not on this site.
