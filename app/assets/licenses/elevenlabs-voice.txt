Voice cues

The spoken workout cues in assets/voice/ were made with ElevenLabs
(https://elevenlabs.io) text to speech, using the ElevenLabs default voices
"Charlie" and "Matilda". Generated 2026-10-08 on the owner's ElevenLabs
account (free plan). Voice by ElevenLabs.

The free plan allows non-commercial use with this credit. Commercial use,
including paid store releases, needs a paid ElevenLabs plan or new
recordings (Reparc handbook D-031, To confirm).

On 2026-10-08 the clips were made again with the eleven_v3 model, in the
same two voices, with a new coach script and spoken exercise names. Each clip
was trimmed and set to one loudness with app/tool/voice/postprocess.py.
