New: the app now tells you when an update is out, warm-ups are logged, and labels no longer break onto two lines.

- **Update alerts.** Reparc checks for a new version once a day and shows it on Home. Tap it, then Download and install. Turn it off in Me → App updates.
- **Warm-ups you can tick off.** A roadmap day now puts its warm-up moves into the workout as warm-up sets. They never count toward volume or records.
- **Home week card** counts the training days you picked.
- **Cleaner labels.** Buttons and titles stay on one line, and pickers use iOS-style arrows.
