Runs and walks without GPS, Health in one tap, and a calmer look.

- **Runs and walks work indoors:** RepArc now counts your steps during a run or walk. When GPS finds no usable fix (indoors, on a treadmill, or with approximate location), the distance comes from your steps and your height, marked as an estimate.
- **Record with steps:** if location is off or not allowed, Run and Walk offer **Record with steps**: no map, no GPS, just time, distance, and steps. Rides still need GPS.
- **Clear GPS status:** the recording screen shows how long it has searched, and how precise the fix is against what it needs ("Weak GPS: ±48 m, needs ±20 m"). If the phone gives RepArc only approximate location, RepArc asks once for precise.
- **Health apps in one tap:** Me → Health apps has **Allow all**. Health Connect or Apple Health opens once with every type; untick any you do not want.
- **Calmer screens:** no more sparkle icons, no rainbow of icon colours on plan goals, shorter text on Health apps and AI connections, and the Home card no longer says "Start workout" twice.
