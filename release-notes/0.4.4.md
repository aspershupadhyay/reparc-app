A new ring clock for your workout, and fixes from testing on a real phone.

- **Two-ring clock:** the outer ring is your whole session, one piece per set. The inner ring fills as your set or rest runs: warm orange for sets, cool blue for rests. Scroll down and it folds into a slim bar, so the clock is always in view.
- **Less text:** the exercise, one short line, and one big button. Plan targets are small tags; fields show kg and reps.

- **Closing the app no longer loses your set:** the set keeps its clock and ends on time, and the workout goes on.
- **No old alerts:** lock-screen alerts from before the app was closed are cleared when it opens.
- **Ramp-up sets show their own reps:** "8 reps at 50%", then "5 reps at 70%", not the working reps.
- Warm-up cardio shows its time ("5:00") instead of "Hold 5:00". An empty workout says Ready, not Done.
- Done sets show only what was saved, with no grey target number.
