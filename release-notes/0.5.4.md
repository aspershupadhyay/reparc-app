A real start for new users, and every chart now opens your own data.

- **New onboarding:** a first launch opens setup, not an empty Home. Answer a few quick questions (goal, experience, days a week, your body, food goal, diet, how active you are) and connect Health in one tap. You finish on your plan, your first workout, and your daily energy and protein targets. Each step can be skipped.
- **Tap any number to see more:** steps, distance, active energy, body weight, workouts, volume lifted, energy eaten, and protein each have their own page: your average or total, how it changed against the period before, a chart, your best day, and every day listed.
- **Charts you can touch:** tap or drag across a chart to read any day. The newest day stands out; a dashed line shows your average and a solid line your target.
- **No more detours:** the steps, distance, energy, and weight cards no longer open the permission or settings pages. If a type is not connected yet, you can connect it on the same page.
