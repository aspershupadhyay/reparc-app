New look: Reparc is now RepArc, with a new logo and a calm opening animation.

- **New logo:** a loaded barbell with round plates whose bar rises in a gentle arc, the "arc" in RepArc.
- **New opening:** the plates load from the left, one bubble flows through the bar like water through a pipe, the plates load on the right, and RepArc fades in. About 2.5 seconds the first time and quicker after that. Tap to skip; with reduced motion on, you see the still logo.
- **Light and dark:** the opening and the Android splash screen follow your phone's theme.
- **New app icon:** the lime barbell on a dark green tile.
- **Name:** the app is now called RepArc on your home screen and inside the app. Your data, backups, and export files are unchanged.
