Recent foods, and small fixes on the live workout.

- **Recent foods:** Add food now lists the last six foods you logged before you search. One tap fills in the food and the amount you had last time.
- **Exercise count:** the finish sheet counts exercises the same way as the plan and History, without warm-up moves. A plan of 7 exercises no longer says 12 when you finish.
- **Workout clock:** between sets the clock is labelled "Workout", not "Ready", because it shows the time since you started.
- **Ring clock:** the moving dot no longer sits on top of the start of the ring for the first seconds of a set or rest.
