Runs, walks, and rides with GPS.

- **Record with GPS:** Workout → Run, Walk, or Ride. See your time, distance, pace (speed on a ride), and your route as you go. Put the phone in your pocket: recording goes on with the screen off, and a notification shows while it records.
- **Precise distance:** RepArc drops weak GPS fixes and GPS jumps, smooths the zig-zag that GPS adds, and does not count standing still. Moving time leaves out stops at lights.
- **Pause and resume:** GPS turns off while paused, and no distance is counted across a pause.
- **After each activity:** distance, average pace or speed, moving and total time, climb, top speed, and splits for each km or mile. Runs, walks, and rides show in History with your workouts.
- **Health apps:** with Write → Workouts on, each run, walk, or ride is saved to Health Connect or Apple Health as that activity.
- **No steps in vehicles:** Live steps stops counting while your phone says you are in a car, bus, or auto, or on a bike, so road bumps are not counted as steps.
- **Private:** routes stay on your phone. RepArc asks for location only while you use it, never "all the time".
