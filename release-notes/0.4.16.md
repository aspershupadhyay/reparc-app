A faster app, most of all once you have months of workouts.

- **History and records:** the workout history and your best lifts now load about 15 to 25 times faster with a long history. Before, every set you logged in a live workout made the app re-read every set you had ever done.
- **New record check:** when you finish a workout, the "new best" check now runs in a moment instead of reading your whole history first.
- **Exercise search:** each letter you type in exercise search is about 10 times faster.
- **No wait on first open:** the exercise list, the food list, and the body map load in the background just after the app opens, so those screens open without a spinner.
- **Database:** indexes on workouts, sets, and meals keep date ranges and lookups quick as your records grow.

Nothing about your data changes: no records are moved or rewritten.
