Indian foods, new bests, and a streak that forgives a missed day.

- **Indian foods:** 87 foods such as roti, paratha, idli, dosa, poha, dal, rajma, chole, biryani, paneer, curd, and chai, with katori, roti, and piece sizes in one tap. Your country's foods come first (Me → Body and goals → Foods from). Values are from USDA; dishes USDA does not have are marked as estimates.
- **New best:** after a workout that beats an earlier best, a card shows your new best. Tap Share to send it as an image.
- **Weekly streak:** Home shows how many weeks in a row you trained. One missed plan day a week is forgiven.
- **One-tap sets:** tick an empty set to save the weight and reps shown.
- **Same as the day before:** on an empty meal day, copy yesterday's foods as planned, with Undo.
- **First time on an exercise:** its card shows how to set up and a "Machine taken? Swap" button. This text is not yet checked by a coach.
