A tidier exercise library with realistic body icons.

- **Body parts first:** Workout → Exercises opens on cards for Chest, Back, Shoulders, Arms, Legs, Glutes, and Core, each with a realistic muscle picture.
- **Then the muscle:** tap Arms to see three cards: Biceps, Triceps, and Forearms. Tap one to see only its exercises.
- **Organised lists:** each muscle starts with the best moves, then groups such as triceps long head, then stretches. Long groups show four moves until you tap **Show more**.
- **One simple filter:** All, Gym, or Home, in place of ten equipment chips. Your choice stays while the app is open, in search, Add exercise, and Swap too.
- **Clear highlights:** every icon shows the anatomy in grey and lights only the muscle it is about.
- **3D body:** the turnable body is now on its own page, from the 3D body card.
