Precise steps, distance, and live step counting.

- **Exact daily totals:** steps and active energy now come from Health Connect's or Apple Health's own daily totals. When your phone and your watch both count the same walk, it is counted once, and a walk across midnight is split between the two days. Before, active energy could count a watch and a phone twice.
- **Distance:** a new switch in Me → Health apps reads how far you walked and ran. Home shows today's distance under your steps.
- **Live steps:** turn it on in Me → Health apps, and today's steps count up as you walk while RepArc is open, with your phone's step sensor. It needs the Physical activity permission (Motion & Fitness on iPhone). Nothing from the sensor is saved or sent.
- **Steps in Analytics:** Analytics → Workout shows your steps a day and distance for the last 7, 30, or 90 days. A day with no data is a gap, never zero.
- **Always fresh:** today's numbers update on their own and when you come back to the app.
