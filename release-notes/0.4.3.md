Your workout now runs on its own: warm-up, rest, then set, rest, set, rest, set.

- **One tap and go:** tap Start workout. Warm-up moves follow each other with no rest, then one rest after the warm-up, then each exercise as set, rest, set, rest, set. The next set starts after a 3-2-1. Prefer to tap? Turn it off in Me → Training → Times and voice.
- **Your times:** choose how long each warm-up move, each set, the rest after the warm-up, and the rest between sets last.
- **3 sets by default** for every new exercise and every new plan.
- **Pyramid sets:** pick Next set: Pyramid and the next set is filled in with 2.5 kg (5 lb) more and 2 fewer reps. Or Same, to repeat the set. You can always change it.
- **Coached when locked, start to finish:** the lock-screen voice now says each set start too, and the workout record catches up when you unlock.
- **A cleaner workout screen:** the clock stays at the top with one big number and one button. Set rows show only what you need. A finished warm-up folds to one line.
- Small fixes: "Paused" shows on Home and Workout, the day page says Continue workout during a workout, and a plan rest such as 1:15 shows in the rest choices.
- Plans you made before keep their set counts. Use + on an exercise for a third set.
