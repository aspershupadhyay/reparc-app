Tap a muscle on a 3D body to find its exercises, and pick moves by the exact part they train.

- **3D body map:** Workout → Exercises opens with a body you can turn. Tap a muscle, then tap its exercise count to open it. Front, Side, and Back snap the view.
- **Three steps to the right move:** body part, then muscle, then the part it trains most. For example Arms → Triceps → Long head, Lateral head, or Heavy presses. The Add exercise sheet in a workout follows the same steps.
- **New muscles:** lower chest, traps, outer hips, and lower abs. Chest is now upper, mid, and lower.
- **Swap for a similar move:** in a workout, an exercise's menu offers moves for the same muscle, closest first, for when the equipment is taken. Your logged sets stay.
- **Smarter search:** gym shorthand works ("bi", "tris", "db", "rdl"), and the best moves come first.
- **History cards** show workout time, total weight lifted, and a PR badge.
- **Fixed:** a workout with no done sets is no longer saved as an empty card. Muscle names no longer break in the middle of a word. Equipment says what you need (for example "Pull-up bar", "Weight plate") instead of "Other", and "Bodyweight" is used everywhere.
