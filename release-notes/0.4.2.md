A new coach voice that keeps talking when your phone is locked.

- **You lead, the coach follows:** tap Start set and Set done. Timed sets, like a plank, end on their own. Want it hands-free? Turn on Start next set on its own and Finish rep sets on time in Me → Training.
- **Every cue, every time:** halfway, ten seconds, a 3-2-1 countdown, "Time! Good set." and "Rest is up". Turn any of them off in Me → Training.
- **Phone locked? Still coached.** The same cues come as notifications in the same voice. On Android a quiet card on the lock screen shows the rest or set clock ticking. Back in the app, they clear away.
- **It knows what's next:** "Next up: Bench press." The panel shows the next set with its weight and reps.
- **New voice:** Charlie and Matilda re-recorded, warmer, all at one volume.
- **Pause really pauses** the set clock and the rest. The screen stays on during a guided workout.
- **Honest numbers:** only measured times are saved. A plan's target time is kept apart from the time you did.
- Android: for an on-time countdown while locked, allow "Alarms & reminders" when the app asks in Me → Training.
