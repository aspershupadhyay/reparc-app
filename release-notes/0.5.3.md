Less clutter on Meal, Home, and Analytics, and the Android back preview.

- **Meal:** add food from each meal's row. The floating **Add food** button is gone, so it no longer covers the end of the day.
- **Meal title:** the date button stays. **Targets** and **Meal times** are now labelled items in one **More** menu, instead of two unlabelled round buttons.
- **Empty days say so:** a day with nothing logged shows one line and the next step (**Plan my day**, **Set targets**), not rows of dashes. Home says "Nothing logged today" with **Log food** until the first food.
- **Analytics:** before your first workout it says what to do next and opens Workout in one tap.
- **Android back gesture:** on Android 13 and later, swiping back shows where you will go before you let go.
