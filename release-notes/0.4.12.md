A guided start for new users and plainer words everywhere.

- **Get your plan in 20 seconds:** new users answer three questions (goal, level, days a week) and get a plan and their first workout. You can skip it.
- **Plain words:** "Stop when 3 more would be hard" instead of "3 reps left", "Add reps, then add weight", "Total lifted", "Hard sets", and "Coach mode". Warm-up sets show a flame.
- **Ramp-up sets show a weight:** "8 reps · 30 kg" instead of "8 reps at 50%".
- **No more rows of zeros:** Analytics says what will appear after your first workout.
- **One plan count:** Home counts plan days from the day your plan started, the same as Analytics.
- **Me starts with your plan.** AI connections moved to Advanced.
- **Large text and screen readers:** food targets on Home are no longer cut off, the Targets button on Meal is no longer covered, and each weight and reps box says which set it is for.
