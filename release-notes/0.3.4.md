Fixes from a full test on a real phone: clearer labels, roadmap days, and messages that close by themselves.

- **Roadmap:** picking training days now saves only when the count matches, and the selected day is visible again. Saving a workout as incomplete no longer skips to the next day.
- **Workout:** the cardio warm-up shows its time, warm-ups read "10 reps", and guides opened during a workout no longer offer to add the move again.
- **Meal:** "Salt" is now "Sodium (mg)", plans say "2 rotis", and screen readers can open a logged food.
- **Everywhere:** messages with Undo close after a few seconds, labels no longer cut off or break, and pickers use iOS-style arrows.
- **Privacy:** the Privacy page now explains the daily update check.
